01 · AI callingflagship

A phone that lives inside five CRMs.

Sales reps shouldn't have to leave their CRM to make a call. I built a WebRTC dialer on SIP.js and embedded it natively in each CRM through its own SDK.

  • Inbound and outbound calls, ring tones, mic selection, noise suppression, live call summaries
  • HubSpot Calling SDK and Pipedrive SDK: handshake, click-to-call, call-state sync
  • Salesforce app built with LWC, Apex and Open CTI. Zoho custom actions. Freshworks workflows.
  • Call agents created automatically, matched to each user's SIP region
